# Ice Breaker evaluation

> Summary: Scores and written feedback for my Ice Breaker "Another World" (Oct 10, 2026).

## Context

- **Speech:** "Another World", my Ice Breaker, given on Oct 10, 2026. Workbook: [speeches/01-ice-breaker-another-world](../../speeches/01-ice-breaker-another-world/)
- **Evaluator:** the club's VP Education.
- **Source:** the digital form on Base Camp (Feedback, then Evaluation Feedback, then Received Feedback). The form was filled in on Oct 26, 2026.

## What worked

My summary of the evaluator's comments:

- Saying "hello" in four languages at the start caught the audience's attention right away.
- I moved across the stage as the story moved, from Uzbekistan on one side to Korea in the center. This showed the change of place well.
- My personal stories showed how life changes with the place we live and the language we speak.
- Gentle eye contact and natural hand gestures made the room feel warm and relaxed.

## What to work on

1. **Notes:** the speech was not fully memorized, so I looked down at my notes sometimes. This pulled attention away from the message. More practice will help me rely less on notes.
2. **Ending:** the speech would be stronger with a punchline at the end that brings the message together. A strong last line gives the audience something to remember.

None given.
