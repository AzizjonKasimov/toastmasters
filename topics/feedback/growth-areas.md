# Growth areas

> Summary: My running list of strengths and things to improve, collected from every evaluation, with what to try next.

## Context

Every evaluation adds to this list. Before each speech, I pick 1 or 2 items and ask my evaluator to watch them. This list is also the input for my Evaluation and Feedback upgrade.

## Things to improve

| Area | From | What to try next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Look at my notes less | Ice Breaker, Oct 10 | Say the opening and closing by heart. Practice the middle from the cue card only. Do one full run with no notes before the meeting. |
| End with a strong punchline | Ice Breaker, Oct 10 | Write the last line first. Make it short and tie it to the main message. Pause before it and slow down. |
| Audience awareness (my lowest score, 3) | Ice Breaker scores | Ask the audience one question, or connect to something said earlier in the meeting. Watch faces, and slow down if people look lost. |

## Strengths to keep

| Strength | From |
| --- | --- |
| A strong, unusual opening (hello in four languages) | Ice Breaker, Oct 10 |
| Using the stage to show how the story moves | Ice Breaker, Oct 10 |
| Personal stories that carry the message | Ice Breaker, Oct 10 |
| Eye contact and comfort (both scored 5) | Ice Breaker, Oct 10 |
