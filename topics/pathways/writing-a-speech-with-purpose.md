# Writing a Speech with Purpose

> Summary: The project about one clear goal written as one sentence, how to build the speech, and what the evaluator scores.

## Context

The second project in Level 1. The Ice Breaker was "tell us about yourself". This project is "choose one goal and hit it". My speech for it is "Give AI a Job" on Nov 7, 2026: [workbook](../../speeches/02-writing-a-speech-with-purpose-give-ai-a-job/).

## The assignment

- A 5 to 7 minute speech on any topic, in any style: persuasive, funny, informative, or anything else.
- The topic must be narrow enough for 5 to 7 minutes. Base Camp's example: "dogs" is too big, "young Golden Retrievers" is the right size.
- Write down the purpose as one sentence before organizing the speech.

## How to build it

1. **Answer three questions:**
   - What is the one most important thing I want to say?
   - Why does it matter to this audience?
   - What do I want people to remember long after the speech?
2. **Choose the general purpose:** inform, persuade, entertain or inspire.
3. **Write the specific purpose:** one sentence from the audience's side, clear and realistic. My form: "After my speech, the audience will ___."
4. **Choose a structure:**
   - Chronological: a story in time order
   - Topical: main topics, then smaller subtopics
   - Spatial: by place, like a trip across a map
   - Causal: cause and effect
   - Comparative: two things side by side
   - Problem and solution: the problem, why it matters, then a fix
   - Particular, general, particular: open with one example, go broad, then come back to the same example at the end
5. **Write it:** an opening that grabs attention, a body where each part has one idea, clear transitions between parts ("First...", "Next...", a new scene, a time of day), and a conclusion that ties it all together.

## What the evaluator scores

Scale: 5 Exemplary, 4 Excels, 3 Accomplished, 2 Emerging, 1 Developing. The form has eight criteria:

| Criterion | What "3, Accomplished" looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Clarity | Clear and easy to understand |
| Vocal Variety | Uses tone, speed and volume as tools |
| Eye Contact | Uses eye contact to engage the audience |
| Gestures | Uses gestures well |
| Interest and Purpose | Interesting, well-built content that supports the specific purpose |
| Comfort Level | Looks comfortable with the audience |
| Specific Speech Purpose | The specific purpose was clear, and the style and structure supported it |
| General Speech Purpose | The speech met the general purpose I chose |

The top of the form has a "Speech Purpose Statement" box. I fill it in with my one sentence before the meeting, so the evaluator knows what to check.
