# Introduction to Vocal Variety and Body Language

> Summary: The project where the evaluation focuses on my voice or my body language, which I choose before the speech.

## Context

The third project in Level 1. Planned for Nov 21, 2026.

## The assignment

- A 5 to 7 minute speech on any topic.
- Before the speech, I tell my evaluator what to focus on: vocal variety, body language, or both. Base Camp says both is a lot for a new member.
- Base Camp suggests filming a practice run, or practicing in front of a mirror.

## The two evaluation forms

- **Vocal variety focus:** the "Speech Profile" form.
- **Body language focus:** the project's own evaluation resource. It has the usual criteria (clarity, voice, eye contact, gestures, audience awareness, comfort, interest) plus two about movement: every movement has a reason, and body language makes the speech stronger.

Both are on Base Camp: digital forms under Feedback, then Evaluation Feedback, and PDFs on the project's first page.

## Vocal variety: four parts

- **Pitch:** how high or low my voice is. A wider range keeps me from sounding flat. Exercise: sing "ahh" from low to high in small steps, then back down. Stay comfortable.
- **Tone:** the feeling in my voice, like happy, sad or excited.
- **Volume:** loud enough for the room. A sudden change, even a whisper, adds emphasis.
- **Pace:** my speed. Too fast is hard to follow, and too slow loses people. Silence and pauses build tension and impact.

## Body language: the parts

- **Posture:** head up, back straight, shoulders back.
- **Stance:** knees relaxed, feet shoulder-width apart.
- **Movement:** stepping away from the lectern adds energy. Move with a reason.
- **Gestures:** four kinds.
  - Descriptive: show size, place or number.
  - Emphatic: show strong feeling, like a hand on the heart.
  - Representational: act out an action, like throwing or picking something up.
  - Prompting: show the audience what to do, like raising my own hand to ask for a show of hands.
- **Culture:** a gesture that is fine in one culture can be rude in another.

## Notes for my choice

My Ice Breaker scores were Vocal Variety 4 and Gestures 4. The evaluator liked how I used the stage to show the story (see the [Ice Breaker evaluation](../feedback/ice-breaker-evaluation.md)). I will choose the focus closer to the date.
