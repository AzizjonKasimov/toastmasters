# Base Camp guide

> Summary: Where things are on Base Camp, what every project page is for, and how to finish a project and a level.

## Context

Base Camp is the Toastmasters website for Pathways: [basecamp.toastmasters.org](https://basecamp.toastmasters.org). It is confusing at first, so this note says what matters.

## The four tabs

- **Paths & Learning:** my path and its projects. Each project is a list of pages in the left sidebar. A green check means a page is done.
- **Achievements:** completed projects and speech logs.
- **Feedback:** "Evaluation Feedback" has the digital evaluation forms. "Received Feedback" shows the forms my evaluators filled in for me.
- **Resources:** PDFs for every project. "My Documents" is where I can upload a photo or scan of a paper evaluation.

## Every project has the same pages

1. **First page:** the resources (PDF checklist, evaluation form, worksheets).
2. **Lesson pages:** short reading and quizzes.
3. **Review and Apply:** a few short questions. Type an answer and click Save on each one.
4. **Complete Your Assignment:** the task checklist and what the evaluator scores.
5. **Complete Your Project:** the Know-Wonder-Learned table and the Speech Log. Fill in the log, then "Save & Email" sends my evaluator a link to the right form.
6. **Before You Move On:** after the speech, click "Complete My Project".

What matters most: give the speech, get the evaluation, then click "Complete My Project". The lesson pages help me prepare and add to my progress percentage.

## Finish Level 1

1. Open "Level 1: Mastering Fundamentals", then "Checklist and Completion". Tick each requirement, then click "Complete My Requirements".
2. Open "Level 1 Completion". Choose the club (Daejeon Toastmasters) and click Submit.
3. The VP Education approves it, and Base Camp sends a notification.

Tip: record my meeting roles and their dates in my Base Camp profile, so the VP Education can see them.

## Notes

- Base Camp does not lock the project order.
- A page with a video can stay unchecked until the video is watched.
