## Context

My path is Presentation Mastery. Level 1 is called "Mastering Fundamentals". It has four speech projects and a few meeting roles. When everything is done, I submit Level 1 on Base Camp and the VP Education approves it.

## The club's rules (from the VP Education, Oct 2026)

- Level 1 needs three more speeches: Writing a Speech with Purpose, Introduction to Vocal Variety and Body Language, and an upgraded version of an earlier speech.
- The upgraded speech is the Evaluation and Feedback project. I pick one earlier speech, apply the feedback I got for it, and give it again.
- The evaluation I already gave (for the VP Education's speech) counts as the evaluator part of Evaluation and Feedback.

Base Camp describes Evaluation and Feedback differently (two new speeches, then an evaluation). The club's version above is the one that counts, because the VP Education approves my level.

Base Camp also lists Timer or Ah-Counter for Level 1. The VP Education did not mention it, so I will ask, or simply do one of them once.

## Plan by date

1. **Sat, Nov 7, 2026:** Writing a Speech with Purpose.
2. **Sat, Nov 21, 2026:** Introduction to Vocal Variety and Body Language.
3. **Next free speech slot after that:** Evaluation and Feedback. Give one of the two speeches again, upgraded with its feedback.
4. **At a meeting where I am not speaking:** Timer or Ah-Counter.
5. **At the end:** tick the Level 1 checklist on Base Camp and submit Level 1. The steps are in the [Base Camp guide](base-camp-guide.md).

## Tips

- Pick topics I am happy to give twice, because one of the two speeches comes back as the upgrade.
- Keep the evaluator's written feedback for both speeches. It is the input for the upgrade.
- Tell each evaluator 1 or 2 things to watch, taken from my [growth areas](../feedback/growth-areas.md).
