# Evaluation and Feedback

> Summary: How my club runs this project (an upgraded earlier speech plus the evaluator role) and what Base Camp still needs.

## Context

The fourth project in Level 1. It teaches two things: how to receive feedback and use it, and how to give feedback to others.

## How my club runs it (this is what counts)

The VP Education confirmed this in Oct 2026:

- **The speech:** pick one of my earlier speeches, apply the feedback I got for it, and give it again as an upgraded version.
- **The evaluator part:** the evaluation I already gave counts.

My plan: give Writing a Speech with Purpose (Nov 7) and Vocal Variety and Body Language (Nov 21), then upgrade the one with the most useful feedback.

## What Base Camp says (for reference)

- Two 5 to 7 minute speeches. Speech 2 uses the feedback from Speech 1. It can be the same speech, a revised one, or a new one.
- The same evaluator should evaluate both speeches. If that is not possible, give the second evaluator the first feedback.
- After both speeches, serve as an evaluator at a meeting.
- Three forms: First Speech, Second Speech (it adds a score for applying the feedback), and Evaluator.

## How to make the upgrade work

- Before the speech, list the 1 or 2 changes I made because of the feedback.
- Tell the evaluator what changed, and give them the earlier feedback.
- Ask the VP Education which form to use. The Second Speech form scores how well I applied the feedback.
