# Meeting roles

> Summary: What each meeting role does, how to do the Timer and Ah-Counter roles, and which roles I have done.

## Context

Level 1 needs these roles: Table Topics speaker, Speaker, Evaluator, and one of Timer or Ah-Counter. Taking roles is also my main way to practice, so I take every role I can.

## My roles so far

| Role | Done | Needed for Level 1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Table Topics speaker | Yes | Yes |
| Speaker | Yes (Ice Breaker) | Yes, once per project |
| Evaluator | Yes, once | Yes |
| Timer | Not yet | Timer or Ah-Counter, once |
| Ah-Counter | Not yet | Timer or Ah-Counter, once |

## The roles

- **Table Topics speaker:** answers a surprise question with a 1 to 2 minute talk. Builds quick thinking.
- **Speaker:** gives a prepared speech for a Pathways project.
- **Evaluator:** gives a 2 to 3 minute spoken evaluation of one speaker and fills in the written form. See [How to give an evaluation](../skills/giving-an-evaluation.md).
- **Timer:** times each speaker and meeting segment, shows the green, yellow and red signals, and reports the times at the end. Builds time management.
- **Ah-Counter:** listens for filler words and sounds like "um", "ah" and "so", notes who said them, and reports at the end. Builds listening skills.

## How to be the Timer

Base Camp has a "Timer Script and Log" PDF in the Level 1 resources. Use it. These are the usual signals:

| Segment | Green | Yellow | Red |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prepared speech, 5 to 7 min | 5:00 | 6:00 | 7:00 |
| Ice Breaker, 4 to 6 min | 4:00 | 5:00 | 6:00 |
| Evaluation, 2 to 3 min | 2:00 | 2:30 | 3:00 |
| Table Topics, 1 to 2 min | 1:00 | 1:30 | 2:00 |

- Before the meeting: get the agenda and each speech's time range.
- At the start: explain the signals in one or two sentences.
- At the end: read out each person's time.

## How to be the Ah-Counter

Base Camp has an "Ah-Counter Script and Log" PDF in the Level 1 resources. Use it.

- At the start: explain which sounds you will count.
- During the meeting: mark each filler word next to the speaker's name.
- At the end: report the counts in a kind, friendly way.
