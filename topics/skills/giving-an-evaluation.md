# How to give an evaluation

> Summary: How to prepare, structure and deliver a 2 to 3 minute speech evaluation.

## Context

Based on the Evaluation and Feedback project on Base Camp. I use it every time I am the Evaluator.

## Before the meeting

- Ask the speaker which project they are doing and what they want me to watch.
- Get the right evaluation form for their project (Base Camp: Feedback, then Evaluation Feedback).
- If I have time, watch the "How to Evaluate" tutorial on Base Camp.

## During the speech

- Take short notes, but mostly watch and listen. Give the speaker my full attention.
- Use the form's criteria to choose what to talk about.

## The spoken evaluation (2 to 3 minutes)

1. **Start with what worked,** with a concrete example.
2. **Give 2 or 3 points to improve, no more.** Say what I saw, and one simple idea to try.
3. **End with encouragement** and one more strength.

How to say it:

- Talk about the behavior, not the person. Use "I" messages: "I noticed you paused before your key line, and it made it stronger." Not: "You were great."
- Say more positive things than negative ones.
- If the speaker met a goal they told me about, say that first.

## After the meeting

- Give the speaker the written form.
- Ask if anything I said needs explaining.
- In a one-on-one talk, first ask how they felt about the speech, then share my view.
